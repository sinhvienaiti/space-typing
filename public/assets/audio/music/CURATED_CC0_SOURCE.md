The mirror re-encodes its bundled CC0 music beds to mono 96 kbps to keep the
package small. In Space Typing these files replace the dated sector/pulse
fallbacks for standard World play. Existing ducking keeps pronunciation,
announcer and warnings above music.

Real-browser listening is still required before calling the audio experience
final.
